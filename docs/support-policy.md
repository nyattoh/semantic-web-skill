# Support policy — proposed

No supported versions or supported jurisdictions have been declared. Read [specification sections 8–10 and 16](product-spec.ja.md).

Choose browser and device coverage from audience, market, current official information, actual usage evidence when available, and available hardware. Record the date and source for each choice. Test representative widths and breakpoint boundaries, orientation, zoom, text growth, and interaction states. Distinguish real devices, browser engines, and emulation.

Select a regional pack by target market, product/service category, audience, medium, and publication date, not by language alone. Japan, the United States, the EU, and the UK are planning folders, not coverage claims. Unknown or unsupported applicability must remain visible.

Provider policies and legal sources require dated review. If current evidence is unavailable, report the verified scope and the unresolved question. A release requires documented results for its declared scope and honest limitations.
