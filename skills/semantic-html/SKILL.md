# semantic-html — draft

Status: design-stage guidance. No executable checks or machine-readable rules are implemented for this module.

## When and inputs

Target markup or content, page purpose, and whether changes are authorized.

## Work and references

Identify what the content means before choosing elements. Distinguish normative content-model constraints from project conventions and semantic judgment.

Read [content models](references/content-models.md); [content patterns](references/content-patterns.md) when needed, then the relevant [specification section 5](../../docs/product-spec.ja.md). Use the [rule-authoring contract](../../docs/rule-authoring.md); the [rule library](../../rules/README.md) is still planned. Do not treat a missing check as passed.

## Output

Structure findings with affected markup, reasoning, primary-source references, and the evidence still needed. Follow [handover guidance](../../docs/handover.md).

## Stop or qualify

Ask for missing inputs only when they change a consequential decision. If the source, environment, implementation, or authorization needed for a conclusion is absent, mark the limitation and stop dependent actions. Do not certify conformance, legal compliance, or release readiness from these draft instructions.
