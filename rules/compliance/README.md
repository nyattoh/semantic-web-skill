# Regional packs — planned

No jurisdiction or industry is supported by a validated pack. The jp, us, eu, and uk folders are design candidates only. See [specification section 10](../../docs/product-spec.ja.md) and [support policy](../../docs/support-policy.md).

A pack must define market, product/service category, audience, medium, time, sources, claim evidence, fixtures, and qualified-review boundary before being described as supported. Do not infer jurisdiction from language alone.
